GT Movies Store

a full stack movie store web app built for CS 2340

This is the link to the website: https://cs2340student.pythonanywhere.com/

Overview

GT Movies Store is a full stack web app where users can browse movies, search by title, leave and manage reviews, and purchase movies through a shopping cart. Administrators can manage users, movies, reviews, and orders.
